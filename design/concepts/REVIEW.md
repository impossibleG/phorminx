# Phorminx concept review

Date: 2026-08-31

Verdict: promote the geometry of `mark-laconic-p-v2` as the sole construction base, then redraw it. Do not ship any study unchanged. The final mark should combine the laconic P's silhouette discipline with one asymmetric, instrument-derived tension cut; it should not inherit the ornamental lyre styling of `mark-tensioned-p-v1`.

All four submitted mark studies fail the literal `UI-ACCEPTANCE` small-size proof because none presents inspected 16, 24, and 32 px monochrome exports. The size findings below are predicted from the submitted geometry and must be confirmed against hand-hinted exports before asset approval.

## Ranking

| Rank | Study | Decision | Why |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `mark-laconic-p-v2.png` | **Promote as construction base** | The only strong black-and-white silhouette, the clearest P, and the least dependent on rendering effects. It is still too generic and its equal vertical slots read as a pause/barcode motif rather than a phorminx. |
| 2 | `mark-laconic-cut-v1.png` | **Mine one idea; reject the mark** | The diagonal lower cut supplies useful tension/asymmetry, but it turns the P into an R/flag shape and the bronze tip is decorative compensation. |
| 3 | `mark-tensioned-p-v1.png` | **Reject** | It joins P and lyre most explicitly, but does so through literal ornament, metallic rendering, crescent finials, flared serifs, and fragile strings. It feels like a classical music institution or luxury game crest. |
| 4 | `mark-stringed-lambda-v1.png` | **Reject** | A literal symmetrical lyre with no P. It is generic to music education/classical culture, over-detailed, and visually costume-like. |
| 5 | `shell-home-reference-v1.png` | **Reject as final; retain only as a spacing reference** | It avoids card clutter, but logo-hidden it is a generic dark utility dashboard. The layout does not yet transcend theme compliance. |

The ranking measures usefulness toward the production identity, not rendering polish. The shell study is a different artifact class, but it ranks last because copying it would establish the wrong visual grammar before route work begins.

## Study-by-study findings

### 1. Laconic P v2

What survives:

- A decisive silhouette and readable initial.
- No Greek costume, microphone, shield, or novelty typography.
- Strong positive/negative mass and easy one-color reproduction.

What fails:

- **Non-generic test:** with the wordmark removed, it could be a publishing, parking, payments, or audio-bars logo. Three equal rectangular slots are an unowned “pause/equalizer/barcode” device.
- **16 px:** three slots become alternating one-pixel gaps/stems or close unpredictably. The counter and slots compete, leaving a comb-like P. There is no asymmetric feature left to identify Phorminx.
- **24 px:** the three slots can remain open only by becoming visually louder than the outer bowl. Equal spacing reads as UI bars, not tensioned strings.
- **32 px:** technically legible, but still generic. The straight slot terminals and perfect semicircle make it feel mechanically generated rather than optically authored.
- The submitted off-white presentation is not a transparent, true monochrome asset proof.

Required correction: retain the outer P proportions, replace the equal slot array with size-specific string cuts, and introduce exactly one quiet asymmetric tension cut in the lower bowl/stem junction.

### 2. Laconic Cut v1

What survives:

- Stronger asymmetry and directional energy than v2.
- The cut suggests tension without drawing a literal instrument.

What fails:

- **Non-generic test:** the lower diagonal reads as an R leg, flag fold, cursor, or construction-company monogram. The bronze triangular cap looks applied after the fact.
- **16 px:** the diagonal collapses to a jagged 1 px staircase; the bronze cap is a stray pixel or disappears; three internal cuts merge. The mark reads as a damaged R/P.
- **24 px:** the diagonal becomes the dominant gesture and pulls the optical center down/right. The tiny triangular accent remains noise rather than identity.
- **32 px:** internal slots become viable, but the R reading remains stronger than the instrument reading. Low-contrast black-on-black rendering conceals the silhouette.
- It depends on metallic edge/light effects and background contrast instead of a clean filled path.

Required correction: use only the idea of an asymmetric cut. The final cut must terminate inside the lower bowl/stem join and must never project like an R leg.

### 3. Tensioned P v1

What survives:

- P and stringed-instrument meanings are both immediately available.
- The outer right curve could inspire a single controlled tension contour.

What fails:

- **Non-generic test:** this is generic “premium classical music” branding. Gold metal, serif feet, crescents, pegs, and literal strings announce the metaphor instead of assuming it.
- **16 px:** all three strings and the gold inner contour alias or vanish; top pegs/crescents merge into a lumpy crown; the foot becomes an unrelated base pixel mass.
- **24 px:** one or two strings may survive inconsistently, while peg/crescent details form noise. The P counter closes visually and the instrument overtakes the letter.
- **32 px:** the silhouette can be recognized, but the gold crescent terminals and flared base still read as ornament. It remains unsuitable for a single-color tray icon.
- It violates the no-luxury-theater and no-faux-historical-ornament tests.

Required correction: none of its literal ornament should transfer. Preserve only the abstract notion of a curve under tension.

### 4. Stringed Lambda v1

What survives:

- Symmetry and a centered axis make the large rendering stable.
- It accurately suggests a lyre family instrument.

What fails:

- **Non-generic test:** it could belong to a conservatory, orchestra, museum, mythology game, or classical record label. It does not encode Phorminx's P or voice-to-form proposition.
- **16 px:** strings, tuning pegs, internal braces, and feet collapse into a dark symmetrical blob with antennae.
- **24 px:** outer arms survive, but interior strings flicker between 0–1 px and the crossbar/pegs merge. It reads as a trophy or gate.
- **32 px:** enough detail survives to read “lyre,” which is precisely the problem: literal category symbol, not ownable product mark.
- Dark iron on black fails silhouette proof before small-size reduction.

Required correction: reject rather than simplify. Simplification would yield a generic lambda/harp, the risk already identified in the blueprint.

### 5. Shell Home reference

What survives:

- It resists card confetti, oversized rounding, gradients, and overt AI theater.
- The navigation is calm and the page has one dominant task.
- Bronze is restrained in area.

What fails:

- **Non-generic test:** hide the wordmark and accent color and this becomes a conventional dark desktop settings shell. Sidebar, large `Ready`, shortcut badge, outlined CTA, and three status rows have no Phorminx-specific composition.
- The microphone, waveform, and Ollama icons are a mixed generic icon-library collage; the llama is especially illustrative relative to the line icons.
- Three unlabeled bronze dots rely on color, do not say ready/degraded/unavailable, and turn operational state into decoration.
- `LOCAL ONLY` repeats privacy as a footer slogan rather than proving it through product behavior.
- `Ready` is oversized but emotionally passive. The page feels empty around it rather than tensioned or composed.
- The outlined `Test dictation` button is visually hollow and resembles a dashboard secondary action despite being the page's sole dominant action.
- The boxed shortcut and wide letter spacing drift toward developer-console styling.
- The embedded title-bar mark is recognizable at roughly 24 px, but its tiny cut is already near closure and has not been proven in monochrome/high contrast.
- The custom title bar is only a picture; snap, system menu, resize, keyboard, DPI, and high-contrast behavior remain unproven.

Required correction: rebuild Home around a status seal/instrument-at-rest composition and a concise readiness sentence. Operational rows must use consistent geometric icons plus explicit text states. Remove the privacy slogan from the footer; state the local contract once where it helps a decision.

## Deterministic final vector construction brief

### Master mark

Build a new one-color compound path named **Tensioned P / Laconic cut**. Do not trace or vectorize the generated PNG.

1. Use a `48 × 48` SVG viewBox with no background, strokes, filters, gradients, masks, blur, texture, or raster content.
2. Set optical bounds to `x=7..41`, `y=4..44`. The mark is vertically centered by visible mass, not merely by the path box.
3. Construct the outer P as one filled silhouette:
   - Stem occupies `x=7..16`, `y=4..44`.
   - Bowl begins at `x=16`, spans to `x=41`, and occupies `y=4..31`.
   - Outer bowl uses a circular/cubic right contour with its widest point centered near `y=17.5`; it is not a perfect font-derived semicircle.
   - Lower bowl returns to the stem at `y=31` through a shallow inward tension curve, not a horizontal line.
4. Cut one primary counter from `x=16..34`, `y=11..25`. Its right boundary echoes the outer bowl with at least `5` units of positive mass at the thinnest point.
5. Inside the counter, add three negative vertical string cuts at the 48-unit master:
   - each cut is `2` units wide;
   - clear gaps between cuts are `2.5` units optically;
   - top terminals align at `y=10` and lower terminals stagger by at most `1` unit to create tension, never a waveform;
   - square terminals receive a `0.5`-unit inward optical cut, not rounded caps.
6. Add exactly one asymmetric negative cut at the lower bowl/stem junction. It begins inside the bowl near `(26, 28)`, tapers toward `(18, 33)`, and ends inside the silhouette. It must not cross the outer contour, create a leg, or make the mark read as R.
7. Use even-odd fill. Validate that the outer silhouette remains one connected component and every negative aperture remains open after rasterization.
8. The master monochrome colors are `#000000` and `#FFFFFF` only. Brand-color applications substitute a single token color; gradients and metallic shading are forbidden.

The coordinate values define the construction regime, not permission to leave optical defects. Any optical adjustment greater than `0.75` viewBox units must be documented in the source asset.

### Size-specific masters

Do not downsample one detailed master for all Windows sizes.

- **16 px:** outer bounds `2..14 × 1..15`; use one centered string aperture exactly `2 px` wide and at least `6 px` tall; omit the other two strings and the lower tension cut if it cannot remain a clean 1 px notch. Minimum positive stem/bowl thickness is `2 px` everywhere.
- **20/24 px:** use two string apertures, each at least `2 px` wide with at least `2 px` positive separation. Keep the lower tension cut only if it occupies at least a `2 × 2 px` open wedge without an R reading.
- **32 px:** use all three apertures, each at least `2 px` wide with `2 px` separation. The asymmetric cut must remain visibly internal and the outer bowl must retain at least `3 px` positive thickness.
- **40/48 px and above:** use the full three-string master and tension cut. Never add pegs, crescents, feet, sound-box ornament, or additional strings at larger sizes.

Every raster master is pixel-hinted at actual size, then inspected at actual size and nearest-neighbor 800% enlargement on black, white, taskbar gray, and high-contrast backgrounds.

### Application and tray assets

- The tray glyph is the monochrome mark only. It uses the 16/20/24 size-specific geometry and Windows system foreground color where available.
- The desktop/application ICO uses an Abyss `#0A0C0D` full-bleed field only where Windows requires a field; it does not add a generic rounded-square container.
- The mark is Limestone `#E8E5DD`. At 48 px and above, exactly one inner string may use Bronze `#A87542`; 16–32 px remains monochrome.
- Export ICO entries at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px with straight-alpha transparency and no baked shadow. Installer, uninstaller, Start, taskbar, Alt+Tab, title bar, and tray derive from the same versioned vector family.

## Final recommendation

Approve a redraw, not an image: **Laconic P v2 silhouette + one internal laconic tension cut + size-specific string apertures**. Reject the literal lyre directions and do not use the current shell image as a component specification. Before implementation consumes the identity, require a real black/white contact sheet at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, and 48 px and a logo-hidden Home screenshot that cannot be mistaken for a generic settings dashboard.
