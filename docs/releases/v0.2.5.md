# Phorminx 0.2.5

Released 2026-10-29

Meeting prep, capture into an investigation from anywhere, Keep awake and a one-click reinstall.

## New

- **Upcoming meetings**: schedule a meeting with a time, a reminder, instructions and important items. Add material as pasted text, PDF, Word files, images or voice notes, let the assistant draft a briefing, and chat with it to prepare. Start the meeting from its plan or from the "Up next" banner.
- **Important items**: pin what matters for a meeting. The assistant always keeps them in mind, and you can add and tick them off during the call, on the Live page or in the companion's Items drawer.
- **Capture into an investigation from anywhere**: press play on an investigation, then use the launcher to add a voice note, the clipboard, an image or screenshot, a link or a note without opening Phorminx.
- **Keep awake**: one switch that stops Windows from sleeping, with an option to keep the screen on too and a timer showing how long it has been on.
- **Reinstall Phorminx**: a button at the bottom of the sidebar, and a Repair Phorminx Start menu shortcut, download the current release and reinstall it with settings reset to defaults. Your history, meetings, messages, investigations and models are kept, and your previous settings can be restored in one click.

## Better

- Meeting commentary has focus styles (balanced, suggest replies, fact-check, track actions, coach), uses earlier context and your prep material, stays quiet when nothing is new, and its cards can be dismissed.
- Turn meeting translation on or off during a meeting, and pick how often commentary runs.
- Start and Finish respond right away, and a finished meeting shows a notice that opens it.
- The Investigations page opens on a list, adds through a + button and keeps the assistant hidden until you press Ask.
- In Messages, click a contact's name for rename, keys, block and remove, and hide the contacts column when you want more room.

## Fixed

- Pressing Dictate inside a Messages conversation no longer opens a blank window and freezes Phorminx.
- Meeting transcripts no longer show the raw capture times inside the text.
